\paragraph{Scope of the 3B result.}
The confirmatory result rests on one 3B model family trained with one recipe, with three seeds per configuration. Its intervals resample questions with the trained models held fixed, so they describe these six models. The non-suppression claim in particular holds for them and not yet for the recipe: with seeds resampled, the interval for $P_c$ crosses the margin, and agents trained without injection used the gold answer \reCleanGoldCost{} points more often than the answer-CE agents. In a second training family that paired clean and attacked continuations, answer CE lowered gold uptake by more than the margin (\cfPairedU\% to \cfPairedCEU\%, descriptive), so the absence of suppression may not hold across recipes. The two configurations are matched on consumed attacks and training-loop time but not on updates; the answer-CE runs completed \updatesCE{} updates and the random-injection runs \updatesR. They also differ in generated tokens and in how much answer supervision each run accepted, so the comparison credits the whole training procedure. Answer CE is the simplest member of a family of training defenses, and other members may learn something different. The agents search about once per question over small HotpotQA distractor pools, so the result concerns how an agent reads a single retrieval.

\paragraph{Payloads.}
Every payload is one sentence appended to the first document of the first search, not a separate poisoned passage that must win retrieval, and evidence position is known to matter \citep{liu2024lost}. The declarative payload is padded with repetitions of the word ``note'', which could make it read as corrupted text; in development, a correct answer stated in the same padded sentence was still used on \dvDeclGoldR\% and \dvDeclGoldCE\% of clean-wrong questions. Only one held-out instruction template moved the agents. The authority claim reached its target on under 2\% of answers even without answer CE, and the override template paraphrases one of the training templates, so whether the effect extends to instructions of other styles is untested. The wrong answer $w(q)$ comes from a fixed heuristic and is not always of the same type as the gold answer. No attack was adapted to the defense \citep{zhan2025adaptive}.

\paragraph{Measurement.}
Exact match penalizes correct answers phrased differently and does not certify that an answer is grounded in evidence. The lenient F1 criterion softens the first problem but not the second.

\paragraph{The 7B comparison.}
The 7B agent is a released checkpoint evaluated on development questions, with a proxy corpus of MuSiQue paragraphs instead of the Wikipedia corpus it was trained with. It differs from our 3B agents in size, training data, corpus, retriever and questions, and from the released 3B in size and training as well as search behavior. Neither contrast isolates the effect of searching more than once. Public questions may also have been seen during pretraining.
